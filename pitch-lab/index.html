SSW here is a simplified concept model. It compares, across one revolution, how long the seams sit in the trip zone and on the separation line on each side of the spin axis. Orientations that look the same on both sides, such as a clean 4-seam or 2-seam, give zero. Use it for the direction of the effect, not its exact size.

Click a trajectory to select it. Outside the Side view, click anywhere else to set the target. Spin is shown at 1/40 speed at the release point and 1/80 in the inset.

Model: drag and lift follow Alan Nathan's baseball trajectory fits; the spin axis is held fixed in space during flight. Breaks follow TrackMan's definitions at the front of the plate, against the straight line along the release velocity: VertBreak includes gravity, InducedVertBreak removes it. Horizontal values (HB, RelSide, PlateLocSide, HorzApprAngle) are + toward the 3B side, the pitcher's right, matching TrackMan's HorzRelAngle (+ toward the RHB box). Tilt is the movement direction on a clock seen from the pitcher (12:00 = pure backspin); SpinAxis = 180° + 30° per hour of tilt. Release defaults are estimated from the pitcher's height and arm slot until you edit them. A pitch's tunnel point is its position 0.175 s after release. The reference is the leftmost Fastball tab, or the leftmost tab when there is no Fastball; gathering re-aims the other pitches so their tunnel points sit on the reference pitch's path.
